Elimination Game using Queue   [Program2]
----------------------------
- N number of player stand in a circle.
- each player has a unique ID from 1 to N.
- Starting from the first player. count K players in a current circle.
- The player at position K is eliminated, and then counting resumes
  from the next player.
- the final output will be only one player remains in the circle.

Sample:
Inputs:
N = 5 {1,2,3,4,5}
K = 3

Output: 4

Explanation:
Round   Q before elimination    eliminated Player   Q after elimination
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1       1 2 3 4 5                    3              4 5 1 2
 2       4 5 1 2                      1              2 4 5
 3       2 4 5                        5              2 4
 4       2 4                          2              4

 Player 4 remains, so that output is 4
