Problem-3: Priority Based Task Scheduling
---------
Task is to rearrange the LL according to the rules
    1. Put all numbers smaller than P at the beginning
    2. Put all numbers grater than or equal to P after them.
Inputs:
    N = 8
   LL E = 7 2 9 1 5 8 3 6
    P = 5  (5 is a partition value)
Output: 2   1   3   7  9   5   8   6


Explain:
           Step-1:  7 2 9 1 5 8 3 6
           Step-2:  Select values smaller than 5
                    2   1   3
           Step-3:  Select values greater than or equal to 5
                    7   9   5   8   6
           Step-4:  2   1   3   7  9   5   8   6


Problem-4: Rotate a LL to the right
---------
Inputs:
    N = 5
    LL E = 101 202 303 404 505
    K = 2 [rotate the list to the right K times]
Output: 404 505 101 202 303

Explain:
           Step-1:  101 202 303 404 505
           Step-2:  First right rotation
                    505 101 202 303 404
           Step-3:  Second right rotation
                    404 505 101 202 303
           Step-4:  404 505 101 202 303
